Chapter 1

Notice Writing

Type: Formal Writing — Notice

What is a Notice?

A notice is a very short but formal piece of writing used by individuals or organizations to convey information to a particular group of people.

Constituents of a Notice

  • Name of the Organisation (In capital letters, centrally aligned)
  • The word ‘Notice’ (In capital letters, centrally aligned)
  • Date of issuing the notice (Month Date, Year e.g. April 10, 20XX or, Date Month Year e.g. 10 April 20XX or 10th April, 20XX aligned at left)
  • Heading/Title (Heading must be short, crisp and catchy giving hint about the message. It should be centrally aligned)
  • Body (Contains purpose of notice including information such as Date, Time, Duration, Place/Venue, Event, Names of people concerned, Charges/Fee, Dead Lines etc)
  • Issuing Authority (Signature, Name, Designation: aligned at left)

Format of Notice

NAME OF THE ORGANISATION
NOTICE

Heading

(Designation)

Important Tips for Writing an Effective Notice

  1. Keep the purpose of notice in mind while drafting the notice.
  2. Keep the target group in mind.
  3. Provide correct and complete information.
  4. Use simple and short sentences.
  5. Use capital letters for details like name of the organisation, events or any important information in the body of the message.
  6. Generate necessary details if the same is not provided.
  7. Keep the notice in a box.
  8. Don't extend the word limit until it is unavoidable.
  9. Don't add any irrelevant or unnecessary details.
  10. Don't add your own opinions.
  11. Use only formal language.
  12. Avoid abbreviations or contractions.
  13. Avoid use of personal pronouns.
  14. Avoid use of slangs or interjections.
  15. Prefer using passive constructions.

  • Format: 2 Marks
  • Body: 3 Marks
