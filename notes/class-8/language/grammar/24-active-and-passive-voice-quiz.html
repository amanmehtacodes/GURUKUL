Quiz 24 — Active and Passive Voice

A fresh set of active-to-passive conversions across tenses, modals, and special sentence types — a follow-up to Lesson 24, not a repeat of it.

How this works: voice-conversion exercises have definite correct answers. Write your attempt in each box, then press the reveal button under each exercise to check your answer against the key.

1.Simple Tenses

Change each sentence into passive voice.

  1. Ravi draws a picture.
  2. The chef cooked a delicious meal.
  3. The manager will approve the leave.
  4. Did the plumber fix the pipe?
Answer Key
  1. A picture is drawn by Ravi.
  2. A delicious meal was cooked by the chef.
  3. The leave will be approved by the manager.
  4. Was the pipe fixed by the plumber?

2.Continuous and Perfect Tenses

Change each sentence into passive voice.

  1. The workers are building a bridge.
  2. She was writing an essay.
  3. They have finished the project.
  4. He had repaired the engine before noon.
Answer Key
  1. A bridge is being built by the workers.
  2. An essay was being written by her.
  3. The project has been finished by them.
  4. The engine had been repaired by him before noon.

3.Modals and Wh-questions

Change each sentence into passive voice.

  1. You should return the books.
  2. She must complete the assignment.
  3. Who wrote this novel?
  4. Where did you buy this bag?
Answer Key
  1. The books should be returned by you.
  2. The assignment must be completed by her.
  3. By whom was this novel written?
  4. Where was this bag bought by you?

4.Imperatives and Prepositional Verbs

Change each sentence into passive voice.

  1. Close the gate.
  2. Don't waste water.
  3. He looked after the injured bird.
  4. They laughed at his joke.
Answer Key
  1. You are requested to close the gate. / Let the gate be closed.
  2. You are advised not to waste water. / Let water not be wasted.
  3. The injured bird was looked after by him.
  4. His joke was laughed at by them.

5.Double Objects and Subject Understood

Change each sentence into passive voice.

  1. The librarian gave me a new book.
  2. His uncle taught him chess.
  3. People call him a hero.
  4. The judge has acquitted the accused.
Answer Key
  1. I was given a new book by the librarian.
  2. He was taught chess by his uncle.
  3. He is called a hero.
  4. The accused has been acquitted.
