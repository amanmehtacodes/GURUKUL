Quiz 17 — Finite and Non-Finite Forms of Verbs

A fresh set of infinitive, participle and gerund exercises — a follow-up to Lesson 17, not a repeat of it.

How this works: these exercises have definite correct answers. Fill in your attempt in each box, then press the reveal button under each exercise to check your answer against the key.

1.Infinitive with 'to'

Fill in the blank with the infinitive with 'to' of the verb in brackets.

  1. She worked hard ___ the exam. (pass)
  2. It is not easy ___ a new language. (learn)
  3. He was too tired ___ any further. (walk)
  4. My aim is ___ a scientist. (become)
  5. They agreed ___ us with the project. (help)
Answer Key

1. to pass · 2. to learn · 3. to walk · 4. to become · 5. to help

2.Bare Infinitive

Fill in the blank with the bare infinitive (no 'to') of the verb in brackets.

  1. I saw him ___ the wall. (climb)
  2. You must ___ your homework before dinner. (finish)
  3. Let her ___ what she thinks. (say)
  4. Why ___ so much time on this? (spend)
  5. The coach made the players ___ extra laps. (run)
Answer Key

1. climb (after 'saw') · 2. finish (after modal 'must') · 3. say (after 'let') · 4. spend (after 'why') · 5. run (after 'made')

3.Present or Past Participle

Fill in the blank with the correct participle form of the verb in brackets.

  1. The ___ leaves covered the ground. (fall)
  2. I found the movie quite ___ (bore)
  3. ___ his bags, he left for the station. (pack)
  4. She has a ___ smile. (charm)
  5. The ___ vase was thrown away. (break)
Answer Key

1. fallen · 2. boring · 3. Packing · 4. charming · 5. broken

4.Perfect Participle

Fill in the blank with the perfect participle form of the verb in brackets.

  1. ___ the door, she went inside. (lock)
  2. ___ his lessons, the boy went out to play. (learn)
  3. ___ the race, she felt proud. (win)
Answer Key

1. Having locked · 2. Having learnt/learned · 3. Having won

5.Gerund

Fill in the blank with the gerund form of the verb in brackets.

  1. ___ is my favourite hobby. (paint)
  2. He avoids ___ junk food. (eat)
  3. I am tired of ___ for the bus. (wait)
  4. She is interested in ___ new languages. (learn)
  5. Thank you for ___ me. (help)
  6. They postponed ___ the meeting. (hold)
Answer Key

1. Painting · 2. eating · 3. waiting · 4. learning · 5. helping · 6. holding

6.Correcting Errors

Correct the non-finite verb error in each sentence.

  1. Read is a good habit.
  2. He denied to take the money.
  3. I am looking forward to see you.
  4. She kept to talk during the movie.
  5. Have finished the exam, he left the hall.
Answer Key
  1. Reading is a good habit. (gerund as subject)
  2. He denied taking the money. ('deny' takes a gerund)
  3. I am looking forward to seeing you. ('look forward to' takes a gerund)
  4. She kept talking during the movie. ('keep' takes a gerund)
  5. Having finished the exam, he left the hall. (perfect participle)
