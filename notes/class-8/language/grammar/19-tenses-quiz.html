Quiz 19 — Tenses

A fresh set of tense exercises across all twelve forms — a follow-up to Lesson 19, not a repeat of it.

How this works: tense exercises have definite correct answers. Fill in your attempt in each box, then press the reveal button under each exercise to check your answer against the key.

1.Simple Tenses (Present, Past, Future)

Fill in the blank with the correct simple tense form of the verb in brackets.

  1. Water ___ into vapour when heated. (turn)
  2. She ___ to Shimla last summer. (travel)
  3. We ___ the winning team next year. (be)
  4. He never ___ his promises. (break)
  5. If it snows, the flight ___ delayed. (be)
Answer Key

1. turns (fact) · 2. travelled (completed past) · 3. will be (future prediction) · 4. breaks (habitual) · 5. will be (future in main clause of a conditional)

2.Continuous Tenses (Present, Past, Future)

Fill in the blank with the correct continuous tense form of the verb in brackets.

  1. Right now, the children ___ in the garden. (play)
  2. At 6 o'clock yesterday, we ___ dinner. (cook)
  3. This time tomorrow, I ___ on a plane to Delhi. (fly)
  4. She ___ Spanish these days. (learn)
Answer Key

1. are playing · 2. were cooking · 3. will be flying · 4. is learning

3.Perfect Tenses (Present, Past, Future)

Fill in the blank with the correct perfect tense form of the verb in brackets.

  1. She ___ her homework already. (finish)
  2. By the time we arrived, the movie ___ (start)
  3. By next year, he ___ this course. (complete)
  4. I ___ never ___ such a beautiful sunset. (see)
Answer Key

1. has finished · 2. had started · 3. will have completed · 4. have, seen

4.Perfect Continuous Tenses (Present, Past, Future)

Fill in the blank with the correct perfect continuous tense form of the verb in brackets.

  1. They ___ for three hours already. (rehearse)
  2. When the bell rang, she ___ for two hours straight. (study)
  3. By evening, he ___ for six hours nonstop. (drive)
Answer Key

1. have been rehearsing · 2. had been studying · 3. will have been driving

5.Identify the Tense

Name the exact tense of the underlined verb in each sentence (e.g. Simple Present, Past Continuous, Future Perfect Continuous).

  1. He has been living in Delhi for ten years.
  2. The train departs at 6 sharp every morning.
  3. They had already left when we reached.
  4. By March, she will have graduated.
  5. We were watching a film when the power went out.
Answer Key

1. Present Perfect Continuous · 2. Simple Present · 3. Past Perfect · 4. Future Perfect · 5. Past Continuous

6.Correcting Tense Errors

Correct the tense error in each sentence.

  1. She is knowing the answer.
  2. I have seen him yesterday.
  3. By the time he arrives, we finish the work.
  4. The sun rise in the east.
Answer Key
  1. She knows the answer. ('know' is a state verb — not normally used in the continuous)
  2. I saw him yesterday. (a definite past time word like 'yesterday' takes the simple past, not present perfect)
  3. By the time he arrives, we will have finished the work. (future perfect, for an action completed before a future point)
  4. The sun rises in the east. (simple present for a universal truth needs the -s form)
