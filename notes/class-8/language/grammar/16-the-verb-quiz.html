Quiz 16 — The Verb

A fresh set of verb exercises — a follow-up to Lesson 16, not a repeat of it.

How this works: verb exercises have definite correct answers. Fill in your attempt in each box, then press the reveal button under each exercise to check your answer against the key.

1.Transitive, Intransitive or Linking?

Underline (mentally) the verb in each sentence and write whether it is Transitive (T), Intransitive (IT) or Linking (L). Mention the object where there is one.

  1. The children laughed loudly.
  2. Riya painted the fence.
  3. The soup tastes delicious.
  4. The old bridge collapsed.
  5. My uncle grew tomatoes in the garden.
  6. The weather turned cold suddenly.
Answer Key

1. laughed (IT) · 2. painted (T) Object: the fence · 3. tastes (L) complement: delicious · 4. collapsed (IT) · 5. grew (T) Object: tomatoes · 6. turned (L) complement: cold

2.Supplying an Object

Complete each sentence with a suitable object.

  1. The farmer sowed ___
  2. She always keeps ___
  3. He forgot ___
  4. They celebrated ___
Sample Answers

This exercise is open-ended. Sample answers: 1. The farmer sowed wheat. 2. She always keeps her promises. 3. He forgot his umbrella. 4. They celebrated the victory.

3.Direct and Indirect Objects

Rewrite each sentence by keeping the direct object before the indirect object (using 'to' or 'for').

  1. She showed me her paintings.
  2. My grandfather told us a story.
  3. The waiter served us dinner.
  4. My friend lent me his bicycle.
Answer Key
  1. She showed her paintings to me.
  2. My grandfather told a story to us.
  3. The waiter served dinner to us.
  4. My friend lent his bicycle to me.

4.Main Verbs and Helping Verbs

Identify the helping verb(s) and the main verb in each sentence.

  1. She has been waiting for an hour.
  2. We must finish this today.
  3. The letter was written by him.
  4. Do you like ice cream?
  5. They will have reached home by now.
Answer Key

1. has, been → waiting · 2. must → finish · 3. was → written · 4. Do → like · 5. will, have → reached

5.Correct Verb Forms

Fill in the blank with the correct form of the verb in brackets.

  1. She ___ (write) three letters yesterday.
  2. The train has already ___ (leave).
  3. He ___ (break) the vase by accident.
  4. They ___ (be) ___ (swim) in the lake since morning.
  5. I ___ (know) the answer already.
  6. The bird has ___ (fly) away.
Answer Key

1. wrote · 2. left · 3. broke · 4. were, swimming · 5. knew · 6. flown

6.Subject Complements

Identify the linking verb and the subject complement in each sentence.

  1. The soup smells wonderful.
  2. My grandmother remains cheerful.
  3. He seemed nervous before the exam.
  4. The leaves turned yellow in autumn.
Answer Key

1. smells → wonderful · 2. remains → cheerful · 3. seemed → nervous · 4. turned → yellow
