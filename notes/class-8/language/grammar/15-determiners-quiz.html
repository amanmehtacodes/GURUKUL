Quiz 15 — Determiners

A fresh set of determiner exercises — a follow-up to Lesson 15, not a repeat of it.

How this works: determiner exercises have definite correct answers. Fill in your attempt in each box, then press the reveal button under each exercise to check your answer against the key.

1.'A' or 'An'

Fill in the blank with 'a' or 'an'.

  1. She is ___ honest woman.
  2. He is ___ European traveller.
  3. I saw ___ one-rupee coin on the road.
  4. This is ___ unique opportunity.
  5. He is ___ MBA graduate.
  6. We waited for ___ hour at the station.
Answer Key

1. an (honest — silent 'h', vowel sound) · 2. a (European — consonant /j/ sound) · 3. a (one — consonant /w/ sound) · 4. a (unique — consonant /j/ sound) · 5. an (MBA — pronounced "em") · 6. an

2.'A' / 'An' / 'The' / (X)

Fill in the blank with 'a', 'an', 'the', or write (X) if no article is needed.

  1. ___ lion is a brave animal.
  2. He is ___ tallest boy in the class.
  3. ___ honesty is the best policy.
  4. We went to ___ market to buy vegetables.
  5. ___ Taj Mahal is in Agra.
  6. My father goes to ___ office by bus every day.
  7. She was elected ___ president of the club.
  8. My brothers are studying in ___ USA.
Answer Key

1. The (whole class) · 2. the (superlative) · 3. (X) (abstract noun, general sense) · 4. the (place visited for other than its primary purpose here — buying vegetables, not the market's own function of selling) · 5. The (famous building) · 6. (X) (place visited for its primary purpose) · 7. (X) (noun complement after 'elected') · 8. the (name of a nation/group of states)

3.Some / Any / Much / Many

Fill in the blank with the correct determiner.

  1. Would you like ___ tea?
  2. I don't have ___ friends in this town.
  3. There isn't ___ sugar left in the jar.
  4. ___ students passed the exam this year.
  5. Do you need ___ help with your homework?
Answer Key

1. some (offer) · 2. any (negative) · 3. much (sugar — uncountable) · 4. Many (countable plural) · 5. any (question)

4.Little / A Little / The Little / Few / A Few / The Few

Fill in the blank with the correct determiner.

  1. I have ___ time, so let's hurry. (hardly any)
  2. She has ___ savings, enough to manage for a week. (some, positive)
  3. He gave away ___ money he had left. (all of it)
  4. There are ___ mistakes in this essay — well done! (hardly any)
  5. I met ___ old classmates at the reunion. (some)
Answer Key

1. little · 2. a little · 3. the little · 4. few · 5. a few

5.Elder / Older / Farther / Further / Nearest / Next

Fill in the blank with the correct word.

  1. My ___ sister works as an engineer. (family)
  2. This building is ___ than that one. (age, not a family member)
  3. He wants to know if there is anything ___ to discuss. (additional)
  4. Mars is ___ from the sun than Earth. (physical distance)
  5. The ___ petrol pump is two kilometres away. (distance)
  6. Turn left at the ___ signal. (position, not distance)
Answer Key

1. elder · 2. older · 3. further · 4. farther · 5. nearest · 6. next

6.Correcting Determiner Errors

Correct the determiner error in each sentence.

  1. I saw a unicorn in my dream.
  2. He is playing on a violin.
  3. Each of the two countries have signed the treaty.
  4. There is not many water left for the plants.
  5. Sun rises in east.
Answer Key
  1. I saw a unicorn in my dream. — correct as written ('unicorn' begins with the consonant /j/ sound, so 'a' is right).
  2. He is playing on the violin. (names of musical instruments take 'the')
  3. Either of the two countries has signed the treaty. ('either', not 'each', for exactly two; and the verb must be singular)
  4. There is not much water left for the plants. (water is uncountable)
  5. The sun rises in the east. (unique object + direction, both need 'the')
