Quiz 22 — The Conjunction

A fresh set of conjunction exercises across coordinative, subordinating, and correlative types — a follow-up to Lesson 22, not a repeat of it.

How this works: conjunction exercises have definite correct answers. Fill in your attempt in each box, then press the reveal button under each exercise to check your answer against the key.

1.Identify the Kind of Conjunction

Name the kind of the underlined conjunction (Coordinative, Subordinating, Correlative, or Compound).

  1. He was late, so he missed the bus.
  2. She stayed home because she was unwell.
  3. He is both clever and hardworking.
  4. I will call you as soon as I land.
  5. Wait here till I return.
  6. She is neither rude nor unkind.
Answer Key

1. Coordinative (Illative) · 2. Subordinating (Cause) · 3. Correlative · 4. Compound · 5. Subordinating (Time) · 6. Correlative

2.Fill in Suitable Conjunctions

Fill in each blank with a suitable conjunction.

  1. He studied hard, ___ he still failed the test.
  2. You must leave now, ___ you will miss the bus.
  3. She sings ___ she dances.
  4. He was punished ___ he had lied.
  5. I will help you ___ you ask me.
Answer Key

1. but/yet · 2. otherwise/else · 3. and · 4. because · 5. if

3.Choose the Correct Subordinating Conjunction

Choose the correct option and type it into the box.

  1. I will not leave ___ (until/unless) you come with me.
  2. She behaved ___ (as if/since) nothing had happened.
  3. He worked hard ___ (so that/because) he might win the prize.
  4. ___ (Although/Because) it was raining, they went for a walk.
  5. He ran fast ___ (lest/as) he should miss the bus.
Answer Key

1. unless · 2. as if · 3. so that · 4. Although · 5. lest

4.Fill in Pairs of Correlative Conjunctions

Complete each sentence with the correct correlative pair.

  1. He is ___ tall ___ handsome.
  2. ___ Rina ___ Priya has finished the project.
  3. He is ___ intelligent ___ he can solve any puzzle.
  4. ___ had the bell rung ___ the students rushed out.
Answer Key

1. both, and · 2. Either, or · 3. so, that · 4. No sooner, than

5.Correcting Conjunction Errors

Correct the conjunction error in each sentence.

  1. He is neither rich or famous.
  2. She is clever but hardworking too.
  3. I do not know that where he lives.
  4. Wait here after I finish my work.
Answer Key
  1. He is neither rich nor famous. ('neither' must pair with 'nor', not 'or')
  2. She is not only clever but also hardworking. ('but...too' is not a valid pair — needs 'not only...but also')
  3. I do not know where he lives. ('that' is redundant before the question word 'where')
  4. Wait here till/until I finish my work. ('after' contradicts the sense of waiting — needs 'till'/'until')
