Quiz 25 — Narration

A fresh set of Direct-to-Indirect Speech conversions across every sentence type — a follow-up to Lesson 25, not a repeat of it.

How this works: narration conversions have definite correct answers. Write your attempt in each box, then press the reveal button under each exercise to check your answer against the key.

1.Assertive Sentences

Change each sentence into indirect speech.

  1. She said, "I am cooking dinner."
  2. He said to me, "I have finished my homework."
  3. The scientist said, "Light travels faster than sound."
  4. My brother said to me, "I will visit you next month."
Answer Key
  1. She said that she was cooking dinner.
  2. He told me that he had finished his homework.
  3. The scientist said that light travels faster than sound. (scientific fact — tense unchanged)
  4. My brother told me that he would visit me the following month.

2.Interrogative Sentences

Change each sentence into indirect speech.

  1. She said to me, "Do you like coffee?"
  2. He said to her, "Where do you live?"
  3. The officer said to him, "Have you got a ticket?"
  4. She said to me, "Shall I close the window?"
Answer Key
  1. She asked me if I liked coffee.
  2. He asked her where she lived.
  3. The officer asked him if he had got a ticket.
  4. She asked me if she should close the window.

3.Imperative Sentences

Change each sentence into indirect speech.

  1. The teacher said to the students, "Open your books."
  2. He said to me, "Please help me with this bag."
  3. She said to her son, "Don't stay out late."
  4. The doctor said to the patient, "Take this medicine daily."
Answer Key
  1. The teacher told the students to open their books.
  2. He requested me to help him with that bag.
  3. She advised her son not to stay out late.
  4. The doctor advised the patient to take that medicine daily.

4.Exclamatory and Optative Sentences

Change each sentence into indirect speech.

  1. She said, "What a wonderful surprise!"
  2. He said, "Alas! I have failed the test."
  3. I said to my friend, "May you live long!"
  4. They said, "Good night, everyone!"
Answer Key
  1. She exclaimed that it was a wonderful surprise.
  2. He exclaimed with sorrow that he had failed the test.
  3. I wished that my friend might live long.
  4. They wished everyone good night.

5.Mixed Practice

Change each sentence into indirect speech.

  1. He said to me, "This is my new bicycle."
  2. She said, "I saw him yesterday."
  3. The captain said to the team, "Play fair and give your best."
  4. He said, "Honesty is the best policy."
Answer Key
  1. He told me that that was his new bicycle.
  2. She said that she had seen him the previous day.
  3. The captain told the team to play fair and give their best.
  4. He said that honesty is the best policy. (proverb-like moral truth — tense unchanged)
