Quiz 26 — Integrated Grammar Practice

Fresh items across sentence reordering, news-headline sense, cloze, editing, and passive-voice process writing — a follow-up to Lesson 26, not a repeat of it.

How this works: Sections 1, 3, 4 and 5 have one definite correct answer — check yourself against the key. Sections 2 and 6 are open-ended; the key gives one strong Sample Answer, not the only acceptable one.

1.Sentence Reordering

Rearrange each jumbled set of words into a correct, meaningful sentence.

  1. the / rang / suddenly / bell / loudly
  2. has / she / finished / already / her / homework
  3. library / every / visits / he / evening / the
  4. were / children / playing / happily / the / garden / in
  5. will / meet / tomorrow / we / station / at / the
Answer Key
  1. The bell rang suddenly and loudly. / Suddenly the bell rang loudly.
  2. She has already finished her homework.
  3. He visits the library every evening.
  4. The children were playing happily in the garden.
  5. We will meet at the station tomorrow.

2.Interpreting a News Headline

Expand each compressed headline into a full sentence, and briefly explain the grammar choice (tense/omission) it relies on.

  1. "Rains lash city, roads flooded"
  2. "PM to visit three states next week"
  3. "Local team wins championship after ten years"
Sample Answer(s)
  1. Heavy rains have lashed the city and flooded the roads. (Headline drops helping verbs and uses the present tense for a recently completed event.)
  2. The Prime Minister is going to visit three states next week. (The infinitive "to visit" stands in for the future "is going to visit" / "will visit.")
  3. The local team has won the championship after ten years. (Present tense "wins" reports a completed event for immediacy; articles are dropped.)

6.Describing a Process in Passive Voice

Read the steps for making a cup of tea, then complete the passage below describing the process in the passive voice (present simple passive).

Steps: boil water — add tea leaves — let it steep for two minutes — strain into a cup — add milk and sugar — stir well.

First, water

Sample Answer

First, water is boiled. Tea leaves are added to it. It is left to steep for two minutes. It is then strained into a cup. Milk and sugar are added, and it is stirred well.
