Quiz 20 — The Adverb

A fresh set of adverb exercises — a follow-up to Lesson 20, not a repeat of it.

How this works: adverb exercises have definite correct answers. Fill in your attempt in each box, then press the reveal button under each exercise to check your answer against the key.

1.Identify the Type of Adverb

Name the type of the underlined adverb (Time, Place, Manner, Degree, Frequency, or Affirmation/Negation).

  1. He left the room quietly.
  2. I have never visited Kerala.
  3. Please wait here.
  4. She is completely exhausted.
  5. We meet occasionally.
  6. He arrived early today.
Answer Key

1. Manner · 2. Negation · 3. Place · 4. Degree · 5. Frequency · 6. Time

2.Adjective to Adverb

Change the bracketed adjective into its adverb form to complete the sentence.

  1. She solved the puzzle ___ (quick)
  2. He greeted us ___ (warm)
  3. The children played ___ in the park. (joyful)
  4. The manager spoke ___ about the delay. (honest)
  5. The team performed ___ this season. (brilliant)
Answer Key

1. quickly · 2. warmly · 3. joyfully · 4. honestly · 5. brilliantly

3.Degrees of Adverbs

Fill in the blank with the correct degree of the adverb in brackets.

  1. She sings ___ than her sister. (well)
  2. Of all the runners, he finished ___ (fast)
  3. My house is ___ to the school than yours. (near)
  4. He performed ___ of all the candidates. (badly)
Answer Key

1. better · 2. fastest · 3. nearer · 4. worst

4.Relative Adverbs

Join each pair of sentences using the relative adverb in brackets.

  1. This is the park. We used to play here. (where)
  2. I remember the day. We first met that day. (when)
  3. Nobody told me the reason. He resigned for that reason. (why)
Answer Key
  1. This is the park where we used to play.
  2. I remember the day when we first met.
  3. Nobody told me the reason why he resigned.

5.Transforming Sentences with 'Too' / 'So...That'

Rewrite each sentence as instructed.

  1. The soup is too hot to drink. (Remove 'too')
  2. He is so short that he cannot reach the shelf. (Use 'too')
  3. This box is too heavy to carry. (Remove 'too')
  4. The music is so loud that we cannot talk. (Use 'too')
Answer Key
  1. The soup is so hot that I cannot drink it.
  2. He is too short to reach the shelf.
  3. This box is so heavy that I cannot carry it.
  4. The music is too loud for us to talk.
