Quiz 18 — Modal Auxiliaries

A fresh set of modal auxiliary exercises — a follow-up to Lesson 18, not a repeat of it.

How this works: modal auxiliary exercises have definite correct answers. Fill in your attempt in each box, then press the reveal button under each exercise to check your answer against the key.

1.Can / Could

Fill in the blank with 'can' or 'could'.

  1. My grandfather ___ speak four languages fluently.
  2. ___ you please pass the salt? (informal request)
  3. When she was young, she ___ run very fast.
  4. ___ I use your phone for a minute, sir? (very polite, formal)
Answer Key

1. can (present ability) · 2. Can (informal request) · 3. could (past ability) · 4. Could (formal, polite request)

2.May / Might

Fill in the blank with 'may' or 'might'.

  1. ___ I borrow your pen for a moment?
  2. He asked if he ___ leave the room early.
  3. ___ you have a long and happy life!
  4. It is very cloudy; it ___ rain heavily. (weak possibility)
Answer Key

1. May (permission) · 2. might (reported speech) · 3. May (wish) · 4. might (weak possibility)

3.Should / Ought to / Must

Fill in the blank with 'should', 'ought to' or 'must'.

  1. Drivers ___ carry a valid licence — it is the law.
  2. You ___ apologise; it would be the right thing to do.
  3. Members ___ renew their subscription before the notice period ends.
Answer Key

1. must (legal obligation, no choice) · 2. should (moral advice) · 3. ought to (formal, situational necessity)

4.Will / Shall / Would

Fill in the blank with 'will', 'shall' or 'would'.

  1. ___ we begin the meeting now? (suggestion)
  2. When he was a boy, he ___ walk five miles to school every day. (past habit)
  3. I ___ definitely finish this by tonight. (strong determination)
  4. The company ___ pay full compensation. (I will see to it — insistence)
Answer Key

1. Shall (suggestion with 'we') · 2. would (past habit) · 3. will (determination) · 4. shall (insistence/promise)

5.Used to / Need / Dare

Fill in the blank with 'used to', 'need' or 'dare'.

  1. There ___ be a big banyan tree at the corner of this street.
  2. You ___ not bring your own lunch; it will be provided.
  3. He ___ not question the coach's decision.
  4. My father ___ collect stamps when he was a boy.
Answer Key

1. used to (something that existed in the past) · 2. need (absence of necessity) · 3. dare (courage) · 4. used to (past habit)

6.Identify the Expression

Name the expression (ability, permission, possibility, advice, obligation, wish, habit, determination, promise, etc.) shown by the modal auxiliary in each sentence.

  1. May you succeed in all your endeavours!
  2. You must submit the form before Friday.
  3. She can swim across the river easily.
  4. He shall receive his prize at the ceremony. (promise)
Answer Key
  1. May — a wish.
  2. must — obligation/compulsion (a deadline with no choice).
  3. can — ability.
  4. shall — a promise.
