Quiz 23 — Agreement of the Verb with Subject

A fresh set of subject-verb agreement exercises across the trickiest rules — a follow-up to Lesson 23, not a repeat of it.

How this works: subject-verb agreement exercises have definite correct answers. Fill in your attempt in each box, then press the reveal button under each exercise to check your answer against the key.

1.Basic Number Agreement

Fill in the blank with the correct form of the verb in brackets.

  1. The dog ___ in the yard. (bark)
  2. The dogs ___ in the yard. (bark)
  3. She ___ to school every day. (walk)
  4. We ___ ready for the exam. (be)
  5. He ___ his homework already. (finish)
Answer Key

1. barks · 2. bark · 3. walks · 4. are · 5. has finished

2.'And', 'Or', 'Nor' Subjects

Fill in the blank with the correct form of the verb in brackets.

  1. Bread and jam ___ his favourite breakfast. (be)
  2. Ravi and Suraj ___ next-door neighbours. (be)
  3. Either the manager or the clerks ___ responsible. (be)
  4. Neither she nor I ___ at fault. (be)
  5. Slow and steady ___ the race. (win)
Answer Key

1. is · 2. are · 3. are (agrees with 'clerks', the nearer plural subject) · 4. am (agrees with 'I', the nearer pronoun) · 5. wins

3.Each, Every, One Of, Along With

Fill in the blank with the correct form of the verb in brackets.

  1. Each of the students ___ a form to fill. (have)
  2. Every boy and every girl ___ present. (be)
  3. One of the windows ___ broken. (be)
  4. The teacher, along with her students, ___ visiting the museum. (be)
  5. Somebody ___ left the door open. (have)
Answer Key

1. has · 2. is · 3. is · 4. is (agrees with 'the teacher', introduced first) · 5. has

4.Collective Nouns and Look-Alike Numbers

Fill in the blank with the correct form of the verb in brackets.

  1. The team ___ its captain today. (choose)
  2. The team ___ among themselves about the strategy. (argue)
  3. Physics ___ my favourite subject. (be)
  4. The cattle ___ near the river. (graze)
  5. Ten thousand rupees ___ a huge amount for him. (be)
Answer Key

1. chooses (team as one unit) · 2. argue (team members considered separately) · 3. is · 4. graze · 5. is

5.Correcting Agreement Errors

Correct the subject-verb agreement error in each sentence.

  1. The list of items are on the table.
  2. Neither of the answers are correct.
  3. The number of mistakes were small.
  4. A pair of scissors are on the desk.
Answer Key
  1. The list of items is on the table. (error of proximity — agrees with 'list', not 'items')
  2. Neither of the answers is correct. ('neither of' takes a singular verb)
  3. The number of mistakes was small. ('the number of' takes a singular verb)
  4. A pair of scissors is on the desk. ('a pair of' treated as one unit takes a singular verb)
