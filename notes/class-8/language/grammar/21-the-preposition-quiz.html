Quiz 21 — The Preposition

A fresh set of preposition exercises across place, time, and the trickiest look-alike pairs — a follow-up to Lesson 21, not a repeat of it.

How this works: preposition exercises have definite correct answers. Fill in your attempt in each box, then press the reveal button under each exercise to check your answer against the key.

1.Prepositions of Place and Direction

Fill in the blank with the correct preposition of place or direction.

  1. The squirrel ran ___ the branch. (along a line)
  2. He hid ___ the curtain. (behind)
  3. The kids are playing ___ the playground. (an area)
  4. Pour the milk ___ the jug. (into a volume)
  5. The lamp hangs ___ the dining table. (above/over)
  6. The dog is sleeping ___ the porch. (on a surface)
Answer Key

1. along · 2. behind · 3. in · 4. into · 5. above/over · 6. on

2.Prepositions of Time

Fill in the blank with the correct preposition of time.

  1. The shop opens ___ 9 o'clock sharp.
  2. We are travelling ___ Monday.
  3. She was born ___ March 1998.
  4. He has been ill ___ Friday.
  5. They worked ___ dawn ___ dusk.
  6. I will be ready ___ five minutes.
Answer Key

1. at · 2. on · 3. in · 4. since · 5. from, to/till · 6. in

3.Above / Over / Below / Under

Fill in the blank with above, over, below, or under.

  1. He hung the mirror ___ the sink.
  2. She held the tray ___ her head.
  3. The scores were well ___ average this term.
  4. They sheltered ___ a large tree during the storm.
  5. His pay is ___ what he expected. (more than)
  6. The matter is ___ discussion right now.
Answer Key

1. above/over · 2. over · 3. below · 4. under · 5. above · 6. under

4.From / With / By

Fill in the blank with from, with, or by.

  1. This bridge was designed ___ a famous engineer.
  2. The vase was decorated ___ tiny beads.
  3. He borrowed a pen ___ his classmate.
  4. She travels to work ___ train every day.
  5. The results differ greatly ___ our predictions.
  6. He was shaking ___ fear.
Answer Key

1. by · 2. with · 3. from · 4. by · 5. from · 6. with

5.Preposition + Word Collocations

Fill in the blank with the preposition that fixedly follows the underlined word.

  1. He is afraid ___ spiders.
  2. She is married ___ a doctor.
  3. This town is famous ___ its handicrafts.
  4. He is not interested ___ cricket.
  5. I am proud ___ my grandmother.
  6. They were satisfied ___ the results.
Answer Key

1. of · 2. to · 3. for · 4. in · 5. of · 6. with

6.Correcting Preposition Errors

Correct the preposition error in each sentence.

  1. He is good in football.
  2. She was angry at her brother for losing the keys.
  3. I am waiting since an hour.
  4. The cat jumped in the box from the floor.
Answer Key
  1. He is good at football. ('good at' is the fixed collocation, not 'good in')
  2. She was angry with her brother for losing the keys. ('angry with' a person, not 'angry at')
  3. I am waiting for an hour. ('for' marks a period of time; 'since' needs a starting point like 'since noon')
  4. The cat jumped into the box from the floor. ('into' shows entry/motion, not rest inside)
