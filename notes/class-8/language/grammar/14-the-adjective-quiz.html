Quiz 14 — The Adjective

A fresh set of adjective exercises — a follow-up to Lesson 14, not a repeat of it.

How this works: adjective exercises have definite correct answers. Fill in your attempt in each box, then press the reveal button under each exercise to check your answer against the key.

1.Kinds of Adjectives

Name the kind of the underlined adjective (Quality, Number, Quantity, Possessive, Demonstrative, Interrogative, Distributive or Emphasizing/Exclamatory).

  1. I have read some milk left in the jug.
  2. Each student must carry their own lunch.
  3. This is my favourite chair.
  4. What a lovely garden this is!
  5. She has five pencils in her bag.
  6. Those mangoes on the tree look ripe.
Answer Key

1. Quantity · 2. Distributive · 3. Possessive · 4. Exclamatory · 5. Number · 6. Demonstrative

2.Order of Adjectives

Arrange the bracketed adjectives in the correct order before the noun.

  1. She wore a (silk, beautiful, red) scarf.
  2. He bought a (round, small, wooden) table.
  3. They live in a (old, charming, stone) cottage.
  4. I saw a (French, tall, young) man at the café.
Answer Key

1. a beautiful, red, silk scarf · 2. a small, round, wooden table · 3. a charming, old, stone cottage · 4. a tall, young, French man

3.Formation of Adjectives (Suffixes)

Form an adjective from the bracketed word and use it to complete the sentence.

  1. This is a very (danger) ___ road to cross.
  2. He gave a (courage) ___ speech before the match.
  3. That was a (fool) ___ mistake to make.
  4. She is a (care) ___ driver.
  5. It was a (wonder) ___ performance.
Answer Key

1. dangerous · 2. courageous · 3. foolish · 4. careful · 5. wonderful

6.Irregular Degrees

Give the Comparative and Superlative degrees of these irregular adjectives.

PositiveComparativeSuperlative
bad
much

old (family)
Answer Key

bad → worse → worst · much → more → most · far → farther/further → farthest/furthest · old → elder → eldest

7.Correct Word Choice

Choose the correct word to complete each sentence.

  1. Ramesh is senior ___ me by two years. (than / to)
  2. Of the two boys, Rahul is the ___. (taller / tallest)
  3. This is the ___ of all the paintings in the gallery. (finer / finest)
  4. She is my ___ sister. (elder / older)
  5. He is ___ prior claim to the property. (a / the) — Sunita has a ___ claim to it. (prior)
Answer Key

1. to · 2. taller · 3. finest · 4. elder · 5. a prior claim

8.Verbs Used as Adjectives

Fill in the blank with the correct participle form of the bracketed verb (present or past).

  1. The (bore) ___ lecture put everyone to sleep.
  2. The (break) ___ vase lay in pieces on the floor.
  3. We watched the (rise) ___ sun from the hilltop.
  4. The (excite) ___ crowd cheered loudly.
  5. The (lose) ___ dog wandered the streets.
Answer Key

1. boring · 2. broken · 3. rising · 4. excited · 5. lost

9.Similes

Complete each simile with the missing adjective or noun.

  1. As white as ___
  2. As busy as a ___
  3. As slow as a ___
  4. As proud as a ___
  5. As cool as a ___
  6. As strong as an ___
Answer Key

1. snow · 2. bee · 3. snail · 4. peacock · 5. cucumber · 6. ox

10.Transformation of Sentences by Degree

Rewrite each sentence in the degree named in brackets, keeping the meaning the same.

  1. Delhi is one of the largest cities in India. (Change to Comparative)
  2. No other boy in the class is as clever as Arjun. (Change to Superlative)
  3. Gold is more precious than silver. (Change to Positive)
  4. The Nile is the longest river in Africa. (Change to Comparative)
  5. Very few cities in India are as clean as Indore. (Change to Superlative)
Answer Key
  1. Delhi is larger than most other cities in India.
  2. Arjun is the cleverest boy in the class.
  3. Silver is not so precious as gold.
  4. The Nile is longer than any other river in Africa.
  5. Indore is one of the cleanest cities in India.
