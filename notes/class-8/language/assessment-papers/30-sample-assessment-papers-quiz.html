Quiz 30 — Sample Assessment Paper (Practice)

A short, fresh fifth practice paper — Reading, Writing and Grammar — a follow-up to Lesson 30's four full papers, not a repeat of any of them.

How this works: objective items (MCQs, matching, rearranging, editing) have one definite correct answer. The comprehension and writing questions are given as Sample Answers.

1.Section-A · Reading

The Road Not Taken (extract) Two roads diverged in a yellow wood, And sorry I could not travel both And be one traveler, long I stood And looked down one as far as I could To where it bent in the undergrowth;

Answer the following questions. (4 marks)

  1. Why was the traveller sorry?
  2. What did the traveller do while standing at the fork?
Sample Answer(s)
  1. The traveller was sorry because he could not travel both roads and be a single traveller on both at once.
  2. He stood for a long time looking down one road as far as he could see, to where it bent into the undergrowth.

Solar energy is energy from the sun that is converted into thermal or electrical energy. It is the cleanest and most abundant renewable energy source available. Modern technology can harness this energy for a variety of uses, including generating electricity, providing light, heating water for domestic, commercial or industrial use, and heating buildings.

Answer the following questions. (3 marks)

  1. What is solar energy?
  2. Name any two uses of solar energy mentioned in the passage.
Sample Answer(s)
  1. Solar energy is energy from the sun that is converted into thermal or electrical energy.
  2. Generating electricity and heating water (also acceptable: providing light, heating buildings).

2.Section-B · Writing

Write a notice informing students of your school about an upcoming inter-house painting competition. Invent necessary details. (4 marks)

Sample Answer

NOTICE
Date: 12 August 2026
INTER-HOUSE PAINTING COMPETITION
All interested students of Classes VI–VIII are informed that an inter-house painting competition will be held on 20 August 2026 in the school auditorium from 10 a.m. to 12 p.m. Students should bring their own painting materials. Interested participants should register their names with their class teacher by 16 August 2026.
— Cultural Committee

Answer Key

Early to bed and early to rise makes a man healthy, wealthy and wise.
