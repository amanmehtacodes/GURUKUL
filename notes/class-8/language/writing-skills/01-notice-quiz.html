Quick format reminder
Every notice needs, in a box: NAME OF THE ORGANISATION (capitals, centred) · NOTICE (centred) · Date (left) · a short catchy Heading (centred) · the Body with all relevant facts · Signature, Name (capitals) and Designation (left). Formal, impersonal language — avoid contractions, slang and personal opinions. See Lesson 1 for the full format and worked examples.
