Quick format reminder
Every informal letter needs: the sender's Address (top left) · the Date, month spelled out in full · a Salutation matching the relationship (family/relatives get "Dear(est) Papa/Mother/Uncle...", friends get "Dear(est) [Name]", never "Dear Friend") · a Body in three or four paragraphs — introduction, main message, closure · a complimentary closure if it fits ("With lots of love", "Hope to see you soon") · the right Subscription ("Yours affectionately/lovingly" for family, "Yours sincerely" for friends) · and your Signature. See Lesson 6 for the full constituents list and the worked example.
