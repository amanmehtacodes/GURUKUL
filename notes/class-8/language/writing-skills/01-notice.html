1. Definition

A notice is a very short but formal piece of writing used by individuals or organizations to convey information to a particular group of people.

2. Constituents of a Notice

  • Name of the Organisation (In capital letters, centrally aligned)
  • The word 'NOTICE' (In capital letters, centrally aligned)
  • Date of issuing the notice (Month Date, Year e.g. April 10, 20XX or, Date Month Year e.g. 10 April 20XX or 10th April, 20XX aligned at left)
  • Heading/Title (Heading must be short, crisp and catchy giving hint about the message. It should be centrally aligned)
  • Body (Contains purpose of notice including information such as Date, Time, Duration, Place/Venue, Event, Names of people concerned, Charges/Fee, Dead Lines etc)
  • Issuing Authority (Signature, Name, Designation: aligned at left)

3. Format of a Notice

NAME OF THE ORGANISATION NOTICE Date (Heading, centred) .......................................................................... .......................................................................... ..................................BODY................................... .......................................................................... .......................................................................... Signature NAME (Designation)

4. Important Tips for Writing an Effective Notice

  1. Keep the purpose of notice in mind while drafting the notice.
  2. Keep the target group in mind.
  3. Provide correct and complete information.
  4. Use simple and short sentences.
  5. Use capital letters for details like name of the organisation, events or any important information in the body of the message.
  6. Generate necessary details if the same is not provided.
  7. Keep the notice in a box.
  8. Don't extend the word limit until it is unavoidable.
  9. Don't add any irrelevant or unnecessary details.
  10. Don't add your own opinions.
  11. Use only formal language.
  12. Avoid abbreviations or contractions.
  13. Avoid use of personal pronouns.
  14. Avoid use of slangs or interjections.
  15. Prefer using passive constructions.

6. Sample Notices

Sample Notice (Lost)

SACRED HEART CONVENT SCHOOL, SOLAN
NOTICE
April 15, 20XX
LUNCH BOX LOST
A brand new Milton lunch box was lost in the playground on April 13 i.e. Saturday. It is red in colour and has a white lid. There is picture of spider man on its lid. Whosoever finds it is requested to return it to the undersigned. A suitable reward is promised.

Geetika Kaushik
GEETIKA KAUSHIK
Roll No. 24, VI A

Hints: Article lost, date, time, place, identification: colour, brand, size, shape, material etc, whom to contact, when to contact, where to contact, promise of reward

Sample Notice (Found)

TAGORE PUBLIC SCHOOL, JAIPUR
NOTICE
May 10, 20XX
GEOMETRY BOX FOUND
A geometry box has been found lying unclaimed in the maths lab. Anyone who has lost it can identify and claim it from the in charge of Lost and Found Department during the activity period within five days of this notice.

Pooja Thakur
POOJA THAKUR
Roll No. 24, VI A

Hints: Article found, person from whom the owner should claim it, date, place and time of claiming

Sample Notice (Meeting)

BLOOMING BUDS PUBLIC SCHOOL, SRI GANGANAGAR
NOTICE
September 5, 20XX
MEETING OF SPORTS CAPTAINS AND HOUSE PREFECTS
All the Sports Captains and House Prefects are hereby informed to attend a meeting with the Sports Teacher on September 9 i.e. Monday sharp at 9:00 am in the Conference Hall to discuss about allocation of the duties for the coming up Annual Sports Meet. Attendance is compulsory.

Harshvardhan Sharma
HARSHVARDHAN SHARMA
(Head Boy)

Hints: Date, time, venue and purpose of the meeting, who is to attend, who will hold the meeting, any other specific instruction

Sample Notice (Events/Competition)

RYAN INTERNATIONAL SCHOOL, NOIDA
NOTICE
August 20, 20XX
SPORTS WEEK
This is to inform all the students that the school is going to celebrate Sports Week from September 1 to September 7. The students who are interested in taking parts in various sports activities are required to give their names to their respective class teachers before August 25.

Minal Bajaj
MINAL BAJAJ
(Sports Secretary)

Hints: Date, time, venue of the competition/event, last date for entries, persons who can apply, person to whom the names are to be submitted, conditions/eligibility criteria, topic, time limit/duration, etc.

Question 1

You are Vikas Bhatnagar, a student of grade VI B of Gems International School, Mumbai. You have lost your colouring kit. Draft a notice in 50 words requesting the finder to return it to you. Invent necessary details. Put the notice in a box.

Model Answer
GEMS INTERNATIONAL SCHOOL, MUMBAI
NOTICE
[Date]
COLOURING KIT LOST
This is to inform all the students that I, Vikas Bhatnagar of Grade VI B, have lost my colouring kit in the school premises. It is a twenty-four shade box with my name written on the inner lid. Whosoever finds it is requested to return it to the undersigned. A suitable reward will be given.
Vikas Bhatnagar
VIKAS BHATNAGAR
Grade VI B

8. Quick Revision Checklist

  • Organisation name in capitals, centred
  • "NOTICE" in capitals, centred
  • Date, left-aligned
  • Catchy heading, centred
  • Body with all relevant facts (5 W's + H as applicable)
  • Signature block (Name in capitals + Designation), left-aligned
  • Entire notice enclosed in a box
  • Formal, impersonal, passive-voice-preferred language
  • Word limit respected (typically 50 words)
