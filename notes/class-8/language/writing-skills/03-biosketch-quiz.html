Quick format reminder
Write the bio-sketch as a single flowing paragraph, not a list — cover date and place of birth, parents, education, career milestones, major achievements with their dates, and (if given) awards, honours, and date/cause of death. Keep facts in roughly the same order as the notes and stay close to 100 words. See Lesson 3 for the full points list and two worked examples.

Question 1Science & Public Service

Use the following information to write a biographical sketch of Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam.

Name: Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam, aerospace scientist, 11th President of India
Date of birth: 15 October 1931, Place of Birth: Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu
Father: Jainulabdeen, a boat owner; Mother: Ashiamma
Education: Diploma in Aeronautical Engineering, St. Joseph's College and Madras Institute of Technology
Career: Worked at DRDO and ISRO, led development of India's Agni and Prithvi missiles, key role in the Pokhran-II nuclear tests of 1998
Known as: the Missile Man of India, the People's President
President of India: 2002–2007
Books: Wings of Fire, India 2020
Honours: Bharat Ratna 1997, Padma Bhushan 1981, Padma Vibhushan 1990
Death: 27 July 2015, while delivering a lecture at IIM Shillong

Question 3Sports

Name: Sachin Ramesh Tendulkar, known as the "Master Blaster"
Date of birth: 24 April 1973, Place of Birth: Mumbai
Father: Ramesh Tendulkar, Marathi novelist and poet; Mother: Rajni Tendulkar
Test debut: 1989 against Pakistan, at age 16
Career: first batsman to score 100 international centuries, first to score a double century in ODIs, most runs in both Test and ODI cricket, captained the Indian cricket team
Awards: Arjuna Award 1994, Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna 1997, Padma Shri 1998, Padma Vibhushan 2008, Bharat Ratna 2014 (youngest-ever recipient)
Retired from all cricket in November 2013, after his 200th Test match; nominated to the Rajya Sabha in 2012
