Quick format reminder
Every article needs a title/heading, a by-line ("By: [Your Name]"), an introduction (may open with a quote, question or striking fact), a well-developed body in three to four connected paragraphs, a winding-up paragraph, and a conclusion with suggestions or a takeaway. Use formal, impressive language — avoid slang. See Lesson 9 for the full CODER writing process and the two worked samples.
