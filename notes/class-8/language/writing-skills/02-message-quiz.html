Quick format reminder
Every message needs: the word MESSAGE (centred) · Date · Time · a brief Salutation to the person it's for · the Body, naming who called and what they said, with any date/time/venue/items mentioned · the Writer's name. Put the whole thing in a box. Keep it under about 50 words, in formal but simple language. See Lesson 2 for the full format and worked example.

Question 1Family

Read the telephonic conversation between Neha and her father, Mr. Khanna. As Mr. Khanna has to leave for work, he leaves a message for his son, Aditya. Write the message in a box in not more than 50 words.

Neha: Papa, can I speak to Aditya?

Mr. Khanna: He has gone for his tuition, beta. Any message for him?

Neha: Yes. Tell him our college fest has been postponed from Friday to next Monday. He needs to collect my entry ticket from the venue office before 6:00 pm on Monday.

Mr. Khanna: Alright, I will tell him.

Question 3Office

Read the telephonic conversation between Ms. Rina Dsouza and Yash, Mr. Kapoor's son. As Yash has to leave for cricket practice, he leaves a message for his father. Draft this message in not more than 50 words. Put the message in a box.

Ms. Dsouza: Could I speak to Mr. Kapoor?

Yash: Papa is not at home right now. May I take a message?

Ms. Dsouza: Yes, please. I am Rina Dsouza, his colleague. Tell him tomorrow's client meeting has been shifted from 10:00 am to 2:00 pm, at the Taj office instead of ours. He should bring the signed contract copies.

Yash: Sure, I will tell him.

Question 5Health

Read the telephonic conversation between Ms. Fatima, receptionist at Apollo Clinic, and Yuvraj. As Yuvraj has to leave for tuition, he leaves a message for his grandmother. Write the message in a box in not more than 50 words.

Ms. Fatima: Good morning, is this Mrs. Sharma's residence?

Yuvraj: Yes, but Dadi is resting right now. Any message for her?

Ms. Fatima: I'm calling from Apollo Clinic. Her check-up appointment has been rescheduled to Wednesday, 11:00 am. Please ask her to bring her previous prescription and reports.

Yuvraj: Okay, I will let her know.
