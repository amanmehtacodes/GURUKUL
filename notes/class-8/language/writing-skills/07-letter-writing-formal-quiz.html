Quick format reminder
An application to the Principal skips the sender's address — start with the receiver's address, then the date, Subject line, salutation ("Sir/Madam"), a body of introduction/main message/closure, "Thanking you in anticipation", "Yours obediently", your signature, Roll No. and Class/Section. A general formal letter includes the sender's address at the top, then date, receiver's address, subject, salutation, the same three-part body, and closes with "Yours faithfully/truly".
