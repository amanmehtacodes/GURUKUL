Quick format reminder
Every speech needs a formal welcome and address to the audience (in correct hierarchy), a self-introduction, an engaging opening (quote, question, anecdote or statistic), a well-organised body covering multiple aspects of the topic, a conclusion that sums up and inspires or calls the audience to action, and a formal closing thanks. See Lesson 10 for the full layout, the golden rules of oratory, and the two worked sample speeches.
