6 fresh story prompts — a follow-up to Lesson 11, not a repeat of it.

How this works: there's no answer key baked into this quiz. Write your story for each prompt in its box, in the same format as Lesson 11 — a clear beginning, middle and end, a suitable title, and a moral at the close.

When you're done, press Generate submission at the bottom. It compiles all six prompts and your answers into one text block — copy that and paste it to an AI (or hand it to a teacher) with a request like "Grade these Class 8 CBSE short stories, 5 marks each."

Quick format reminder
Every story needs a suitable title, a clear beginning that sets up characters, time and place, a middle that develops a conflict or problem, an interesting end, and a moral or message. Write mainly in the Past Tense, use sequence markers and cohesive devices, and add descriptive adjectives, adverbs and figures of speech. See Lesson 11 for the full writing points and the worked example, "The Midas Touch".
