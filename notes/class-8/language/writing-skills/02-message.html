1. Definition

A message is an informal but important short piece of writing used to convey some information or give instructions to a particular person who is not available at the moment. Usually a message is based on a telephonic conversation between the caller and the receiver. The content of the message is meant for the person for whom the call was made. In certain cases, the message writing can be based on certain hints provided in the question.

2. Constituents of a Message

  • The word 'Message'
  • Date
  • Time
  • The name of the person to whom the message is directed
  • Message including the name of the person who called
  • Writer's name

3. Format of a Message

MESSAGE Date Time Salutation .......................................................................... ...............................Message to be conveyed.................... .......................................................................... Writer's Name

4. Steps for Writing a Message

  1. Rough Draft: Enlist the important things to be included in the message by keeping the following questions in mind according to the message.
    • Who called?
    • Who is the message for?
    • What is the message?
    • When and what is the reader supposed to do?
    • What is the reader supposed to send/bring?
  2. Final Draft: Write the word 'Message'.
  3. Write the date and time.
  4. Write a brief salutation. (Dear papa/mom, or just Papa/Mom or some other name)
  5. Write the body of the message.
    • Include the name of the person who called.
    • Write the message.
    • Don't forget to mention date, time, venue, things (if any) as a part of the message.
    • Write the name of the person who writes the message.
  6. Put the message in a box.

5. Marking Scheme

ComponentMarks
Date and Time1 Mark
Name of the person to whom the message is directed/addressed½ Mark
Content (message, spellings, grammar)3 Marks
Writer's name½ Mark
Total5 Marks

6. Sample Message (Worked Example)

Read the following conversation between Mr. Ajeet Sood and Abhimanyu, Mr. Gupta's son.

Mr. Sood: Hello, is it 296432758?

Abhimanyu: Yes, may I know who is talking?

Mr Sood: This is Ajeet Sood, your father's colleague, here. Could I speak to Mr. Gupta?

Abhimanyu: I am sorry. Papa is not at home. If you have any message, please leave it with me.

Mr. Sood: Tell your father that his meeting with the Japanese delegate scheduled at 9:30 at Kings Hotel has been postponed by two hours. Now the meeting is at 11:30 am. Tell your father to reach the office at 9:30 instead of going to Kings Hotel directly.

Abhimanyu: Ok sir, I will convey the message to my father.

Mr. Sood: Thank you.

As Abhimanyu has to leave for his school, he leaves a message on a message slip. Draft a message in not more than 50 words on behalf of Abhimanyu. Put the message in a box.

MESSAGE
April 24, 20XX
7:00 a.m.
Papa

Your colleague, Mr. Ajeet Sood called up to inform you that your meeting with the Japanese delegate scheduled at 9:30 at Kings Hotel has been postponed by two hours. Now the meeting is at 11:30 am. You must reach the office at 9:30 instead of going to Kings Hotel directly.
Abhimanyu

8. Practice Questions

Question 1

Read the telephonic conversation between Vishal and his sister, Vibhuti. As Vibhuti has to leave for her guitar classes, she leaves a message for her mother. Write the message in a box in not more than 50 words.

Vishal: Can I speak to mom?

Vibhuti: Mom has gone to market. Do you have any message for her?

Vishal: Yes. My friend, Aakash's mother is admitted to City Hospital as she has met with an accident. I am going to City Hospital with a few friends. I will come home by 7:00 pm.

Vibhuti: Ok. I will inform her.

Model Answer
MESSAGE
[Date]
[Time]
Mom

Vishal called up to inform you that his friend Aakash's mother has met with an accident and has been admitted to City Hospital. He has gone to City Hospital with a few friends and will come home by 7:00 pm.
Vibhuti

9. Quick Revision Checklist

  • The word "MESSAGE" written clearly (right-aligned, as in the format above)
  • Date and Time both included
  • A brief salutation naming who the message is for
  • Name of the person who called, included in the body
  • All key facts covered — what, when, where, and what the reader should do
  • Writer's name at the end
  • Entire message enclosed in a box
  • Word limit respected (typically 50 words)
