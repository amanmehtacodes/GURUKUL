1. Definition

Factual description is a formal piece of writing often used in writing biographies, describing objects, places, ideas or events. As the name suggests, a factual description is a systematic description of facts about a person, object, place or event. The writer describes the facts in detail covering various aspects of the person or the thing being described so as to enable the reader to get a comprehensive idea about the subject being described. A factual description is generally written in the form of paragraph.

2. Important Aspects of Factual Description

  • Objective description of the facts
  • Realistic and vivid description
  • Comprehensive description (covering various aspects)
  • Systematic description: One aspect at a time (For example: If you are describing the physical features of the object, you must not switch to its internal value)
  • Formal, correct language, no use of slangs
  • Variety in the use of vocabulary
  • No repetition
  • The length of factual description may vary depending on the purpose. For examination purpose, the length of a factual description will be limited, 80-100 words.

3. Marking Scheme

This marking scheme applies to a factual description of a person, object, place or event alike.

ComponentMarks
Format (Introduction, Description, Conclusion)1 Mark
Content2 Marks
Language & Expression2 Marks
Total5 Marks

4. Describing People

While describing people the following points are considered important.

  • Physical traits
  • Personality trait
  • Occupation/Profession
  • Routines/habits
  • Hobbies
  • Philosophy of life
  • Other people's opinion about the person
Physical Traitsage, height, body, structure, face, complexion, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, hair colour and type, clothes, etc.
Behaviour/Personality Traitactive, ambitious, creative, curious, talkative, out spoken, soft spoken, polite, rude, loving, caring, affectionate, kind, compassionate, strict, humble, proud, introvert, extrovert, shy, narrow/open-minded, amiable, friendly, weird, understanding, humorous, jovial, fun-loving etc.
Hobbiessinging, dancing, reading, surfing on net, painting, gardening, playing cricket/football/hockey etc.
Describing People · Sample: My Favourite Teacher
Mr. Sharma is my favourite teacher in the school. He teaches us science. He is a tall, handsome and well-built man. He is 40 years old. He is always smartly and formally dressed. He is very well-behaved, soft-spoken and polite. He is kind and compassionate. Sometimes he also cracks witty jokes. He explains the concepts very well. He is fond of reading books and playing golf. Everyone in the school appreciates and likes him. I really admire him a lot. May he live long!

Question 1

Describe your uncle/father/grandfather in 80-100 words.

Model Answer
My grandfather, Mr. Ramesh Verma, is seventy-two years old. He is of medium height, with silver hair and a warm, wrinkled face that lights up whenever he smiles. He always wears simple cotton kurtas and walks with the help of a wooden cane. A retired schoolteacher, he is calm, patient and extremely disciplined in his daily routine — he wakes up before dawn, goes for a walk, and spends his evenings reading the newspaper or tending to his small garden. He is fond of gardening and classical music. He believes that honesty and hard work are the keys to a good life. Everyone in our family adores and respects him deeply.
Question 2

Describe your best friend in about 80-100 words.

Model Answer
My best friend, Priya, is a cheerful and lively girl of my age. She is of medium height, with curly hair and a bright smile that never seems to fade. She is extremely talkative, friendly and outspoken, yet very caring and understanding towards everyone around her. She studies in my class and is exceptionally good at mathematics, often helping me with difficult problems. Priya loves dancing and painting, and spends her weekends practising classical dance. She is honest, humble and always ready to help others in need. Whenever I feel low, her sense of humour instantly cheers me up. I consider myself fortunate to have a friend like her.
Question 3

Describe a celebrity you like the most in about 80-100 words.

Model Answer
A.R. Rahman is the celebrity I admire the most. He is a renowned Indian music composer and singer, popularly known as the "Mozart of Madras". He is soft-spoken, humble and deeply spiritual by nature, despite his enormous success. He has composed music for numerous Bollywood and Hollywood films and has won several National Film Awards, two Academy Awards, and two Grammy Awards for his work. He is known for blending traditional Indian sounds with modern global music styles. Beyond his talent, he is admired for his humility, generosity and dedication to charitable causes. His journey from a struggling musician to a global icon truly inspires me.

5. Describing Objects

While describing objects the following points are considered important.

  • The name of the object
  • Model/year of manufacture
  • Brand/Company
  • Guarantee/warranty
  • Shape/structure
  • Colour and material
  • Contents/parts
  • Sizes available/dimension
  • Utility/capacity/functions/operational details
  • Availability
  • Price
  • Your remarks about the object/product
  • Your experience with the object/product
Describing Objects · Sample: Kindle
I have recently purchased a Kindle. Kindle is a small hand-held electronic device for reading books, which has been developed by online retailer Amazon. It is the latest model, Paper White, which has a high contrast touch screen which allows me to read even in bright sunshine with no glare. The device is extremely light weight and portable. I buy books as Kindle versions of 'e-books' from the Amazon website. In addition, I can also download PDFs of any sort of document, which makes this an ideal way of reading for work and study. It has a six-inch screen to read from and a small key board. It has built-in wifi and 3G connection to download books on to it. I can adjust the text size in it. It has battery life of at least a month if wifi is turned off. It also has a page-turning function to get the feel of reading a real book. It can store up to 1400 books. The books can be categorised or stored as collections. I can also add my own annotations to the text. It has also got a built-in Oxford English Dictionary. It also has the feature of password protection. Kindle has made my reading experience much more joyful and beneficial. I love it.
Question 4

You have purchased a new laptop. Describe it including all its features.

Model Answer
I have recently purchased a new laptop from Dell, model Inspiron 15. It has a sleek, silver-coloured metal body and weighs just under two kilograms, making it easy to carry around. It features a fifteen-inch full-HD display, an eleventh-generation Intel Core i5 processor, 8 GB RAM and a 512 GB solid-state drive, which makes it extremely fast and efficient for both study and entertainment. It has a backlit keyboard, a fingerprint scanner for quick login, and a battery life of up to eight hours. It also comes with built-in Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and multiple USB ports. The laptop comes with a one-year manufacturer's warranty. I am extremely satisfied with its performance and design.
Question 5

Describe your wrist watch in not more than 100 words.

Model Answer
My wrist watch is a Titan analog watch that my parents gifted me on my birthday. It has a round, stainless-steel case with a sleek black leather strap. The dial is white with silver hour markers and slim black hands, giving it an elegant, formal look. It is water-resistant up to thirty metres and comes with a one-year warranty from the company. The watch is lightweight and comfortable to wear throughout the day. Besides telling the time accurately, it also has a small date display window. I wear it every day to school, and it perfectly complements both my casual and formal outfits. I take great care of it as it is very dear to me.

6. Describing Places

While describing places the following points are considered important.

  • Name of the place
  • Its location
  • Dimension/area
  • Surrounding
  • Detailed description in terms of structure, entrance, exit, boundary, walls, height, length, breadth, paintings, flooring, furnishing, ventilation, light, capacity etc.
  • Importance
Describing Places · Sample: My School Library
My school library is a well set-up library. It is a very big library on the ground floor of the school. It consists of several book stands and cabinets. Books are arranged according to categories in these book stands and cabinets. It has a wide range of books on diverse subjects, story books, comic books, subject books, reference books, journals, encyclopedia, biographies and magazines. At the entrance there is the librarian's desk. There are rows of tables and chairs in the center of the library for students to sit. Another section is the reference section of the library house for teachers. All the students visit library as per their classroom visit schedule. Library cards are to be carried while visiting library. Our librarian is very helpful in locating and selecting books as per our needs. We are allowed to borrow one book at a time and the records for the same are maintained by the librarian. We need to ensure that the books are not damaged by us and are returned on time.
Question 6

Describe a historical monument you have recently visited.

Model Answer
I recently visited the Taj Mahal in Agra, one of the Seven Wonders of the World. It is located on the southern bank of the river Yamuna and was built by the Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife, Mumtaz Mahal. The monument is made entirely of white marble and is famous for its perfect symmetry, intricate carvings, and inlay work of precious stones. It stands within a large, well-maintained garden complex with a long reflecting pool leading up to it, flanked by four tall minarets. The monument attracts millions of tourists from across the world every year. Watching the marble glow in the morning sunlight was truly a breathtaking experience I will always remember.
Question 7

Describe your city in about 80-100 words.

Model Answer
I live in Jaipur, popularly known as the "Pink City" of India. It is the capital of Rajasthan and is famous for its magnificent forts, palaces and vibrant culture. The city is named for the pink-coloured buildings in its old quarter, a colour scheme adopted to welcome the Prince of Wales in 1876. Jaipur is home to several historical landmarks such as the Hawa Mahal, Amber Fort and City Palace, which attract tourists from around the world. The city is also known for its colourful bazaars, delicious Rajasthani cuisine, and traditional handicrafts. Jaipur beautifully blends its rich royal heritage with modern development, making it one of the most visited cities in India.

7. Describing Events

While describing events the following points are considered important.

  • Name of the event
  • Date, time, venue
  • Purpose/Theme
  • Organisers
  • Mode of entry or Conditions for entry
  • Chief guests/ participants/ people who conducted the event
  • Inauguration
  • What happened in the event
  • Prizes/awards/recognitions/honours
  • Audience's opinion about the event
Describing Events · Sample: The Earth Day
The Earth Day is an annual event celebrated on April 22. Worldwide, various events are held to demonstrate support for environmental protection. This year Earth Day was celebrated in my school with great pomp and show. The day began with the morning assembly where one student talked at length about the importance of celebrating this day. Another student recited a poem on the importance of saving natural resources. Our worthy Principal also addressed the students and shared her views on the importance of saving trees and forests. The day continued with some competitions such as Poem Recitation Competition, Poster Making Competition, and Essay Writing Competition etc. We also planted saplings in the school premises. The winners of various competitions were given attractive prizes and certificates. The event taught us about the importance of saving the blue planet in all its varied manifestations.
Question 8

Your school has recently organised a musical event "Confluence". Describe the event.

Model Answer
My school recently organised a musical event called "Confluence" in the school auditorium. The event was organised by the Music Department to celebrate and showcase the musical talents of students from different classes. Entry was open to all students and parents with a pass distributed in advance. The event was inaugurated by our Principal, who lit the ceremonial lamp along with the chief guest, a renowned playback singer. Students performed a variety of solo and group songs, classical dance pieces and instrumental performances throughout the evening. The winners of the singing and dance competitions were awarded trophies and certificates. The audience thoroughly enjoyed the performances, and the event ended on a joyous and memorable note.
Question 9

Describe the Annual Function held in your school.

Model Answer
The Annual Function of my school was held recently in the school auditorium with great enthusiasm. The event was organised by the teachers and the student council to celebrate the achievements of the academic year. It began with the lighting of the ceremonial lamp by our Principal and the chief guest, followed by the school prayer. Students performed various dance, drama and musical items that they had rehearsed for weeks. The Principal addressed the gathering and shared the school's achievements over the year. Prizes and certificates were distributed to students who excelled in academics, sports and extracurricular activities. Parents who attended the event were highly appreciative of the students' talents and the smooth organisation of the entire programme.
Question 10

Describe Parents Teacher Meet held recently in your school.

Model Answer
A Parents Teacher Meet was held recently in my school to discuss the academic progress of students. The meeting was organised by the school administration and was attended by parents and subject teachers of every class. Parents were given specific time slots to meet each teacher individually and discuss their child's performance, strengths and areas that needed improvement. Report cards were distributed, and teachers offered helpful suggestions on how parents could support their children's studies at home. The Principal also addressed the parents briefly, highlighting the school's upcoming events and academic plans. Most parents found the meeting extremely useful and appreciated the teachers' guidance and the school's transparent approach towards students' overall development.

8. Quick Revision Checklist

  • Description is objective, realistic and vivid — not vague or exaggerated
  • Covers all the relevant points for that category (person / object / place / event)
  • One aspect described at a time, in a logical, systematic order
  • Formal, correct language — no slang, no unnecessary repetition
  • Written as flowing prose in a single paragraph, not a list
  • Introduction, description and conclusion all present
  • Stays close to the word limit (typically 80-100 words)
