  Chapter-2                                    Bio-Sketch
A bio-sketch or biographical sketch is a brief but formal piece of writing. It is the description of a person’s life
including all the signiﬁcant aspects such as birth, family, education, occupation, achievements, social status,
death (if the person is no more). All the major events are described along with important dates or years. It may be
the description of a celebrity or a common person. It is a brief description in about 100 words, although the word
limit may vary according to the notes provided for the description. The key events along with the dates are
provided in the question.

write a bio-sketch of Kalpana Chawla.
Name: Kalpana Chawla, ﬁrst Indian born woman in space
Date of birth: 17 March, 1962, youngest of four children
Ofﬁcial Date of Birth: 1 July, 1961, to allow her to become eligible for

Place of birth: Karnal, Haryana
Education: Bachelor of Engineering in Aeronautical Engineering form

Moved to USA in 1982, Master of Science degree in Aerospace
Engineering from the University of Texas at Arlington in 1984, second
Masters in 1986 and a PhD in aerospace engineering in 1988 from the
University of Colorado Boulder.
Career: In 1988, began working at NASA where she did computational
ﬂuid dynamics (CFD) research on vertical and/or short take-off and
landing (V/STOL) concepts, held a Certiﬁcated Flight Instructor rating
for airplanes, gliders and Commercial Pilot licenses for single and
multi-engine airplanes, seaplanes and gliders, naturalized U.S.

First space mission : November 17, 1997, as part of the six-astronaut crew that ﬂew the Space Shuttle Columbia

In 2000, selected for her second ﬂight as part of the crew of space shuttle Columbia on the ill fated ﬂight STS-107.
Death: Space Shuttle Columbia disaster on February 1, 2003, killed along with the other six crew members,
when the Columbia disintegrated over Texas during re-entry into the Earth's atmosphere

The ﬁrst Indian-born woman in space, Kalpana Chawla was born on March 17, 1962 at Karnal, Haryana, but her
ofﬁcial date of birth was altered to 1 July 1961 to allow her to become eligible for the matriculation exam. She
was the youngest of four children. After getting a Bachelor of Engineering degree in Aeronautical Engineering
from Punjab Engineering College,India, she moved to the United States in 1982 and obtained a Master of Science
degree in Aerospace Engineering from the University of Texas at Arlington in 1984. Chawla earned a second
Masters in 1986 and a PhD in aerospace engineering in 1988 from the University of Colorado Boulder. In 1988,
she began working at NASA, where she did computational ﬂuid dynamics (CFD) research on vertical and/or
short take-off and landing (V/STOL) concepts. Chawla held a Certiﬁcated Flight Instructor rating for airplanes,
gliders and Commercial Pilot licenses for single and multi-engine airplanes, seaplanes and gliders. After
becoming a naturalized U.S. citizen in April 1991, she applied for the NASA Astronaut Corps. She joined the
corps in March 1995 and was selected for her ﬁrst ﬂight in 1996. Her ﬁrst space mission began on November 17,
1997, as part of the six-astronaut crew that ﬂew the Space Shuttle Columbia ﬂight STS-87. Chawla was the ﬁrst
Indian woman to ﬂy in space. In 2000, Chawla was selected for her second ﬂight as part of the crew of ﬂight STS-
107 of the space shuttle Columbia. Chawla died in the Space Shuttle Columbia disaster which occurred on
February 1, 2003; she was killed, along with the other six crew members, when the Columbia disintegrated over
Texas during re-entry into the Earth's atmosphere.
