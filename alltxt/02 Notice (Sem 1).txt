Chapter-1                                                                        Notice
A notice is a very short but formal piece of writing used by individuals or organizations to convey
information to a particular group of people.

                                                           Constituents of a Notice
       ¥ Name of the Organisation (In capital letters, centrally aligned)
       ¥ The word ‘Notice’ (In capital letters, centrally aligned)
       ¥ Date of issuing the notice (Month Date, Year e.g. April 10, 20XX or, Date Month Year e.g. 10 April
         20XX or 10th April, 20XX aligned at left)
       ¥ Heading/Title (Heading must be short, crisp and catchy giving hint about the message. It should be
         centrally aligned)
       ¥ Body (Contains purpose of notice including information such as Date, Time, Duration, Place/Venue,
         Event, Names of people concerned, Charges/Fee, Dead Lines etc)
       ¥ Issuing Authority (Signature, Name, Designation: aligned at left)

                               Important Tips for writing an effective Notice
1.        Keep the purpose of notice in mind while drafting the notice.
2.        Keep the target group in mind.
3.        Provide correct and complete information.
4.        Use simple and short sentences.
5.        Use capital letters for details like name of the organisation, events or any important information
          in the body of the message.
6.        Generate necessary details if the same is not provided.
7.        Keep the notice in a box.
8.        Don’t extend the word limit until it is unavoidable.
9.        Don’t add any irrelevant or unnecessary details.
10.       Don’t add your own opinions.
11.       Use only formal language.
12.       Avoid abbreviations or contractions.
13.       Avoid use of personal pronouns.
14.       Avoid use of slangs or interjections.
15.       Prefer using passive constructions.

                                         Sample Notice (Lost)
                                 SACRED HEART CONVENT SCHOOL, SOLAN
                                               NOTICE
  April 15, 20XX
                                                   LUNCH BOX LOST
  A brand new Milton lunch box was lost in the playground on April 13 i.e. Saturday. It is red in colour and has a white
  lid. There is picture of spider man on its lid. Whosoever ﬁnds it is requested to return it to the undersigned. A suitable
  reward is promised.

  Geetika Kaushik
  GEETIKA KAUSHIK
  Roll No. 24, VI A


     Hints: Article lost, date, time, place, identiﬁcation: colour, brand, size, shape, material etc, whom to
     contact, when to contact, where to contact, promise of reward
