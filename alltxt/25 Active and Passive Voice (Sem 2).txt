Chapter-11                            Active and Passive Voice

Read the following pair of sentences carefully.
     1. He reads a book.
     2. A book is read by him.
The meaning of both sentences is the same. However, the structure is different. In Sentence 1, the subject of the
verb 'reads' is 'He'. Here, the subject 'he' is the active doer of the verb/action. Hence, the sentence is in active
voice.
In sentence 2, the subject of the verb 'is read' is 'A book'. Here, the subject is not the active doer of the verb/action.
It is the passive receiver of the action. Hence, the sentence is in passive voice.
Active Voice: When the verb shows that the subject does something, it is said to be in Active Voice.
Passive Voice: When the verb shows that something is done to the subject, it is said to be in Passive Voice.

Now read the following examples carefully and observe how sentences are changed from active to passive
voice.
                       Active Voice                                           Passive Voice
        Boys are playing cricket.                             Cricket is being played by boys.
        Children made a noise.                                A noise was made by children.
        She teaches us maths.                                 We are taught maths by her.
        This box contains sweets.                             Sweets are contained in this box.
        He knows me.                                          I am known to him.


The following steps are followed while converting a sentence from active to passive voice.
    1. The object of the active verb is put in the place of subject.
    2. The active verb is changed in passive form by using a form of 'be' according to tense of the active voice
        and the III form of the verb. Other auxiliaries are also used according to the tense.
    3. The passive verb should be in agreement with the object which is brought forward.
    4. The subject is kept after the passive verb and it is preceded by the preposition 'by', thus converting the
        subject into agent or doer.
    5. In most of the cases preposition 'by' is used before agent. In certain cases preposition other than 'by' is
        used.

                Important Rules to follow while changing the Active Voice into Passive Voice

    1. Don't change the tense.
    2. The passive voice of future continuous tense and all perfect continuous tenses is not possible.
    3. Don't change the type of sentence.
    4. Subject-verb agreement must be followed in passive voice too.

                 13. Sentences with Transitive Verbs with Double Objects

Transitive verbs with double objects can be changed into passive voice by changing either object into
subject. However, it is preferable to make indirect object the subject of the passive verb.

Active Voice: She teaches us maths.
Passive Voice: (Direct Object) Maths is taught to us by her.
               (Indirect Object) We are taught maths by her.
Active Voice: My father gifted me a watch.
Passive Voice: (Direct Object) A watch was gifted to me by my father.
               (Indirect Object) I was gifted a watch by my father
