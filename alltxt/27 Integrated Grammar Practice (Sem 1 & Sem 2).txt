newspaper has various kinds of news items covering areas such as politics, sports, business, entertainment,
advertisements, notices, articles, letters to the editor etc and hence is a very rich source of vocabulary. The
language used in news headlines is made compact by using non-ﬁnite verbs, phrases and passive structures in
order to provide maximum information using minimum number of words in an impressive way. The news
headlines are often catchy and spicy to attract the readers’ attention.

                                          Points to Remember
 1. Use of Simple Present Tense: Simple Present Tense is excessively used in the news headlines. In news
    headlines, Simple Present Tense is used to denote an action that somebody has done in recent past or likely to
    do in near future. The reporting of a news headline in the present tense can be done in simple present, simple
    past or simple future depending on the news. For report in simple past, past time reference is important.
 2. Use of Inﬁnitive with to: In news headlines, Inﬁnitive with ‘to’ is used to denote an action that someone is
    going to do in near future.
 3. Use of Past Participle: In news headlines, Past Participle is used to denote a verb in passive form to suggest
    that something has been done to some subject.
 4. Use of Present Participle: In news headlines, Present Participle can be used to denote an action of present
    continuous tense or a future action.
 5. Use of Simple Past Tense: While reporting a news headline, if past time is added, it is written in simple past
     tense.
 6. Voice : The report of a news headlines may be written in active or passive voice depending on the
    sentence structures used in the report.
