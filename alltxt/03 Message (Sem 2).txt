 Chapter-1                                                                   Message

A message is an informal but important short piece of writing used to convey some information or give
instructions to a particular person who is not available at the moment. Usually a message is based on a telephonic
conversation between the caller and the receiver. The content of the message is meant for the person for whom the
call was made. In certain cases, the message writing can be based on certain hints provided in the question.

                                                   Constituents of a Message
     Æ     The word ‘Message’
     Æ     Date
     Æ     Time
     Æ     The name of the person to whom the message is directed
     Æ     Message including the name of the person who called
     Æ     Writer’s name

                                                             Format of Message
                                                                                 MESSAGE
    Date
    Time
    Salutation

    ..............................................................................................................................................................................

    ...............................................................Message to be conveyed........................................................................

    ..............................................................................................................................................................................


    Writer's Name


                                                 Steps for writing a Message
   1. Rough Draft: Enlist the important things to be included in the message by keeping the following
         questions in mind according to the message.
             Æ Who called?
             Æ Who is the message for?
             Æ What is the message?
             Æ When and what is the reader supposed to do?
             Æ What is the reader supposed to send/bring?
   2.    Final Draft: Write the word 'Message'.
   3.    Write the date and time.
   4.    Write a brief salutation. (Dear papa/mom, or just Papa/Mom or some other name)
   5.    Write the body of the message.
             Æ Include the name of the person who called.
             Æ Write the message.
             Æ Don't forget to mention date, time, venue, things (if any) as a part of the message.
             Æ Write the name of the person who writes the message.
   6.    Put the message in a box.

Example:
Read the following conversation between Mr. Ajeet Sood and Abhimanyu, Mr. Gupta's son.
Mr. Sood: Hello, is it 296432758?
Abhimanyu: Yes, may I know who is talking?
Mr Sood: This is Ajeet Sood, your father's colleague, here. Could I speak to Mr. Gupta?
Abhimanyu: I am sorry. Papa is not at home. If you have any message, please leave it with me.
Mr. Sood: Tell your father that his meeting with the Japanese delegate scheduled at 9:30 at Kings Hotel has been
postponed by two hours. Now the meeting is at 11:30 am. Tell your father to reach the ofﬁce at 9:30 instead of
going to Kings Hotel directly.
Abhimanyu: Ok sir, I will convey the message to my father.
Mr. Sood: Thank you.
As Abhimanyu has to leave for his school, he leaves a message on a message slip. Draft a message in not
more than 50 words on behalf of Abhimanyu. Put the message in a box.


                                                                 MESSAGE
    April 24, 20XX
    7:00 a.m.
    Papa

    Your colleague, Mr. Ajeet Sood called up to inform you that your meeting with the Japanese delegate
    scheduled at 9:30 at Kings Hotel has been postponed by two hours. Now the meeting is at 11:30 am.
    You must reach the ofﬁce at 9:30 instead of going to Kings Hotel directly.

    Abhimanyu


Most often used phrases/sentence structures:
     1.   .........................rang up/called up to inform you that..................................
     2.   You should/You are supposed to reach/call/bring/take/pick up.............................
     3.   He/she will.........................................................................
     4.   Also call him/her.
     5.   Take his/ her.......................................................... with you.
     6.   The next ......................will be ........................... at .................on ........................
