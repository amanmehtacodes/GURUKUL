 Chapter-4                                      Formal Letters
Formal letters, also known as ofﬁcial letters, are the letters written to the head of an institution for a particular
purpose. The language, style and tone of formal letters are formal and polite with no personal references,
opinions or expression. Although there is no speciﬁed word limit for a formal letter, it should stick to the point and
justify the purpose of writing the letter. The content should be well organized in a logical sequence using
appropriate cohesive devices.

      Æ   Job Applications

      Æ   Letters to CEO/Bank Manager/Director
      Æ   Letters to Publishers
      Æ   Complaints

4. Subject or Heading: It brieﬂy states the purpose of writing the letter. It is generally highlighted or underlined.
Examples:
    Æ Subject: Application for School Leaving Certiﬁcate
    Æ Subject: Complaint against the Postman
    Æ Subject: Frequent power failures
    Æ Subject: Bad quality and insufﬁcient supply of water
5. Salutation: Salutation is the formal and polite way we address the recipient of the letter. It is written below the
subject. Example: Dear Sir/Madam, Dear Sirs, or only Sir/Madam
6. Body of the letter: It is the main part of the letter which contains the message or the information meant to be
conveyed through the letter. Although there is no hard and fast rule regarding the number of paragraphs it should
contain, it is usually written in three or four paragraphs depending on the content to be written. The body has three
parts:
(a) Introduction: It includes the introduction of the sender and purpose of writing the letter.
(b) Main Message: The second paragraph is the main message or information to be conveyed. It gives a detailed
description of the purpose of writing the letter. This part should be well organized using appropriate cohesive
devices. If the content of the letter is long, there may be two paragraphs for the main message.
(c) Closure: The third paragraph concludes the letter. It usually states what is expected of the receiver. It may also
include some suggestions related to the purpose of the letter. It also includes a complimentary closure such as
‘Thank you” or ‘Thanking you in anticipation’.
7. Subscription: It is a polite way of closing the letter. Read the examples of subscription given below.
   Æ For Principal
      Ø Yours obediently
   Æ For others
      Ø Yours faithfully/truly
8. Signature: The sender’s signature is written just below the subscription

You are Akriti Singla of Grade VI A of Strawberry Fields Public School, Pune. Write a letter to your
Principal requesting him/her to exempt you from taking Ofﬁce Tests as you have to go to Mumbai to

  reality show ‘Indian Idol’ telecast on Sony TV, and got selected for the Theatre Round. The shooting of the

  Thank you.
  Yours obediently
  Akriti Singla
  Roll No. 7
  Grade VI A

                                                   Formal Letter-Sample
House No. 315, Street No. 5
New Azaad Colony
Moga
October 25, 20XX
The President
Municipal Committee
Moga
Subject: Regarding insanitary conditions of the locality
Sir
I am a resident of New Azaad Colony. Through this letter of mine, I wish to draw your kind attention towards
the insanitary conditions prevailing in our locality.
Our colony, though located in the heart of the city, is the most neglected and worst maintained colony in the
town. There are no sweepers to clean the streets. There are no provisions for throwing the garbage.
Consequently, the heaps of garbage are scattered here and there spreading foul odours in all directions.
These heaps are the breeding places for ﬂies and mosquitoes. The sewage system of the colony is in a bad
state. There are cracked drainage pipes and overﬂowing manholes making the streets dirty and stinking. The
dirty water leaking all the time is a great threat for the health of the residents of the colony.
This state of affairs needs your urgent attention and the needful action before epidemics like malaria and
cholera break out. You are humbly requested to look into the matter and do the needful.
Thanking you in anticipation
Yours truly
Dheeraj Yadav
