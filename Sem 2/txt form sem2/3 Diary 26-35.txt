                                                       What is Diary Entry?
It is human nature to share one’s personal experiences with near and dear ones. Many people keep a record of their
day to day experiences in a diary. Diary entry is a very personal and informal way of writing. It is a means of keeping
record of day to day events, happenings, activities, personal experiences, plans, hopes, opinions, fears, joys, sorrow,
aims, targets, achievements, gains, loss, mistakes, apologies and many such human feelings.
Remember, it is not just enlisting what happened chronically. As it is a personal record, it is often fused with lots of
emotions, personal expressions and opinions. It is the description of how the diary keeper feels about a particular
incident that happens in his life or that he witnesses or experiences.
Although personal, the record may be about any theme such as friends, teachers, classmates, school teachers,
neighbours, animals, social or political issues, or issues related to one’s area, country or world. Some people treat
their diary as their close friend and converse with it as if it were a human being. They even give a name to their diary.

               How to Make Diary Entry Creative and Effective
Æ Use your imaginative powers.
Æ Use a variety of vocabulary.
Æ    Explain in detail how you feel about a particular event or incident.
Æ    Use appropriate words to express your feeling.
Æ    Use appropriate tense to describe past events or future planning.
Æ Don't deviate from the topic.
Æ Use poetic language if you can.
Æ Avoid spelling or grammatical mistakes.
Æ Add pictures, stickers, sketches, doodles, smileys etc to match your feelings.

Sample-I You have topped your class in ﬁnal examination. Write your diary entry expressing your
feelings.

Tuesday, 30th March 20XX
10:00 p.m.

Dear Diary
It was the happiest day of my life. The result of ﬁnal examination was declared, a moment I was eagerly waiting
for since the day ﬁnal examination ended.

I have stood ﬁrst in the ﬁnal examination and scored 99% marks. My joys knew no bounds when the result was
declared. As soon as my name was announced as the topper of the class, all the students started shouting
jubilantly. They all started congratulating me. I was on the seventh sky at that moment. My class teacher also
congratulated me and patted my back. I also thanked her and all other teachers for teaching so well.

 My parents, grandparents and siblings were all extremely happy to know about my glorious success in the ﬁnal
examination. They all were proud of me. I had burnt midnight oil for this day. My parents were congratulated by
the Principal, my teachers, friends and neighbours. I went to temple to say ‘Thanks’ to God. We offered sweets
at temple and distributed the same among our near and dear ones. I also thanked my mother who has always
helped me in studies. In the evening my friends and I went to a restaurant to celebrate my grand success. I will
always cherish the memories of this day. I hope I continue to achieve success in years to come as well.

Good night!

 Advika
Sample-II On your way to school, you saw a car run over a little puppy. Write your diary entry expressing
your feelings about the incident.
 Friday, Novemeber 26, 20XX
 9:30 p.m.

Dear Diary
I had a terrible experience today. It was a day of mixed emotions: anger, pity, satisfaction and what not.

Today afternoon while I was coming back from my school, I saw a speeding car run over a liitle, innocent
puppy. I was shocked to see the plight of the puppy. The driver of the car seemed to be a man without heart as he
did not even bother to stop his car and look at the bleeding puppy whose leg was badly wounded under his car. I
was mad with fury and wanted to hit the driver hard, but at that time I thought it more important to take care of
the injured puppy. I felt really pained to see how indifferent people have become to others’ pains be it a human
being or an innocent animal. No one bothered to take the puppy to a vet.

I took pity on the poor thing and rushed to a veterinarian. The doctor was a nice man who immediately cleaned
the wounded leg and put bandage around it. He gave puppy an injection to reduce the pain. He did not even
charge a single penny when I told him that the puppy was a stray dog. He also gave the puppy milk and
buiscuits. I was overwhelmed to see the kind treatment given by the vet. I brought the puppy home. My uniform
was all blood stained, but I had a great sense of pride and satisfaction that I had saved the life of a poor puppy.
The incident has set me thimking about providing a shelter to the poor stray animals.

See you later.

Prateek
