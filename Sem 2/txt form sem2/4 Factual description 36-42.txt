 Chapter-3                             Factual Description
Factual description is a formal piece of writing often used in writing biographies, describing objects, places,
ideas or events. As the name suggests, a factual description is a systematic description of facts about a person,
object, place or event. The writer describes the facts in detail covering various aspects of the person or the thing
being described so as to enable the reader to get a comprehensive idea about the subject being described. A factual
description is generally written in the form of paragraph.

                                       Describing Places
While describing places the following points are considered important.
 Æ Name of the place
 Æ Its location
 Æ Dimension/area
 Æ Surrounding
 Æ Detailed description in terms of structure, entrance, exit, boundary, walls, height, length, breadth,
       paintings, ﬂooring, furnishing, ventilation, light, capacity etc.
 Æ Importance

                Describing Places: Sample: My School Library




My school library is a well set-up library. It is a very big library on the ground ﬂoor of the school. It consists of
several book stands and cabinets. Books are arranged according to categories in these book stands and cabinets. It
has a wide range of books on diverse subjects, story books, comic books, subject books, reference books,
journals, encyclopedia, biographies and magazines. At the entrance there is the librarian’s desk. There are rows of
tables and chairs in the center of the library for students to sit. Another section is the reference section of the
library house for teachers. All the students visit library as per their classroom visit schedule. Library cards are to
be carried while visiting library. Our librarian is very helpful in locating and selecting books as per our needs. We
are allowed to borrow one book at a time and the records for the same are maintained by the librarian. We need to
ensure that the books are not damaged by us and are returned on time.

While describing events the following points are considered important.
  Æ Name of the event
  Æ Date, time, venue
  Æ Purpose/Theme
  Æ Organisers
  Æ Mode of entry or Conditions for entry
  Æ Chief guests/ participants/ people who conducted the event
  Æ Inauguration
  Æ What happened in the event
  Æ Prizes/awards/recognitions/honours
  Æ Audience's opinion about the event

                   Describing Events: Sample: The Earth Day




The Earth Day is an annual event celebrated on April 22. Worldwide, various events are held to demonstrate
support for environmental protection. This year Earth Day was celebrated in my school with great pomp and
show. The day began with the morning assembly where one student talked at length about the importance of
celebrating this day. Another student recited a poem on the importance of saving natural resources. Our worthy
Principal also addressed the students and shared her views on the importance of saving trees and forests. The day
continued with some competitions such as Poem Recitation Competition, Poster Making Competition, and
Essay Writing Competition etc. We also planted saplings in the school premises. The winners of various
competitions were given attractive prizes and certiﬁcates. The event taught us about the importance of saving the
blue planet in all its varied manifestations.
